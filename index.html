Privacy Policy for MusicPlayer

Last updated: August 26, 2026

DivineTech ("we", "us") operates the MusicPlayer mobile application (the "App"). This page explains what information the App accesses and how it is used.

Information We Access

Music library (on your device only). The App requests permission to read your device's audio files (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) so it can scan and play the songs already stored on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or stored on any server we operate.

Notifications. The App requests notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) solely to display the media playback control notification while music is playing.

Modify system settings. If you choose to set a song as your ringtone, the App requests the WRITE_SETTINGS permission to do so. This is only used when you explicitly trigger that action.

Locally stored data. Playlists, favorites, custom album art, and any lyrics you add are stored only in local app storage on your device. We do not have access to this data and it is not backed up to any server we control.

Advertising

This App displays ads served by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies), including banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. Unity Ads may collect device identifiers (such as advertising ID) and usage data to serve and measure ads, and to enable optional features like rewarded ads that temporarily pause advertising.

Unity's data practices are governed by their own privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

You can review or reset your advertising ID, and control ad personalization, in your device's system settings.

Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads shown in the App use Unity's standard ad experience, and no ads are specifically targeted at children.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not operate any backend server that stores your music, playlists, or listening activity. The only third party involved is Unity Ads, described above.

Your Choices

  • You can revoke media/storage or notification permissions at any time in your device's App Settings.
  • You can opt out of interest-based advertising via your device's ad settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads on most Android devices).

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
